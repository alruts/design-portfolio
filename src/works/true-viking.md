intro: Microsite design created in Figma for True Viking, an Icelandic perfume brand. The fragrance and bottle were already on the market, but the client needed a digital presence to showcase the product. I designed the website in Figma and developed a visual concept linking the scent to Icelandic nature and Viking mythology. To support this, I directed a photoshoot of the bottle, placing it within moss, mountains, ice, fire, and lava. These elements frame the product in a raw, adventurous landscape, reinforcing True Viking’s bold and untamed identity. Designed at Kontor Reykjavík under the guidance of Art Director Alex Jónsson.
