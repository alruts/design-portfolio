intro: Developed the brand identity and digital experience for Everen Analytics, a specialist actuarial analytics platform. The project focused on creating a clear, confident and professional visual language for communicating complex financial data. Combining a distinctive geometric identity with a refined digital interface, the design translates technical analytics into an accessible and intuitive experience. The visual system balances precision and clarity through considered typography, a restrained colour palette and structured layouts, supporting a brand built around smarter insights and stronger decisions.
