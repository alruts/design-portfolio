<p>
  Rather than proposing new offerings, the team conducted observations, interviews
  and workshops to surface a more foundational issue: the studio lacked internal
  alignment and a clearly articulated identity. The initial brief was broad and
  solution-focused; reframing it was the first intervention.
</p>
<p>
  The outcome was a dynamic culture handbook built in Figma and a sculptural object
  for the studio space — tools designed to surface purpose, values and aspirations,
  encouraging reflection, dialogue and collective ownership. Developed as part of
  a Master's in Strategic Design and Entrepreneurship at the Royal Danish Academy.
</p>
