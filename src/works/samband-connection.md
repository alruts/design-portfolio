intro: Designed an exhibition for 3daysofdesign in Copenhagen, showcasing outstanding products by nine Icelandic designers whose work is developed, manufactured, and sold in Scandinavia. The exhibition was organized by Eyjólfur Pálsson (Epal) in collaboration with Iceland Design and Architecture, Business Iceland, and the Icelandic Embassy in Copenhagen. Collaborated with renowned furniture designer Guðmundur Lúðvík.
