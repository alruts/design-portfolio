<p>
  Display objects were mounted on custom dark grey MDF podiums — warehouse-pallet
  shaped and rectangular column forms — referencing the building's history as a
  200-year-old shipping hub between Denmark and the North Atlantic. The podiums
  recede into the space, letting each exhibit take centre stage.
</p>
<p>
  Labels used clean, proportioned typography with QR codes linking to manufacturer
  websites. Developed alongside Eyjólfur Pálsson and designer Guðmundur Ludvík.
</p>
