<p>
  Directed the photoshoot for Hopp's new car rental service, producing imagery
  that continues to be used across their social media. Managed the Instagram
  account and developed materials across web, social media, print and presentations
  — all within the brand's existing guidelines.
</p>
<p>
  Work carried out while at Kontor Reykjavík, in collaboration with
  creative director Sigrún Gylfadóttir.
</p>
